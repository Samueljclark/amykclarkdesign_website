## Where I work

Hyde Park, Indian Hill, Mariemont, Terrace Park, Wyoming, and Anderson Township are regular ground, and so is everything between them. The houses are not interchangeable and neither are the windows. A 1920s Hyde Park sunroom with original steel casements is a different problem from a new build in Anderson with a two-story wall of glass, and the same treatment would be wrong in both.

## What that means in an older house

Older Cincinnati houses rarely give you a square opening. Plaster moves, sills are out of level, and the trim was run by hand a century ago. I take every measurement in the room rather than assuming it off a plan, and build the treatment to the opening that is actually there. That is what decides whether an inside mount reads as intentional or slightly off, and it is why I never order anything from a photograph.

## The whole textile layer

Drapery, blinds and shades, upholstery, pillows and cushions. Fabric comes from the lines I represent, chosen for how it will hang and wear rather than how it looks folded on a table. I am not a full-service interior design firm. I own the textile layer of your room and work alongside whoever owns the rest.

If a designer, builder, or project manager is already running the house, that is an ordinary way to work here. If it is just you and a room that has never looked right, that is ordinary too.
