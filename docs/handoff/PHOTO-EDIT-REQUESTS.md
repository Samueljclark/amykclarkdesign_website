# Photo edit requests — Amy K Clark Design website

**Prepared 2 August 2026 for a retouching pass.**

This is a complete inventory of the photographs currently in the website
project, with a note on where each one appears and what a final edit could
improve. It is written to be read on its own — you should not need access to
the website's files to use it.

## How to read this list

Each entry gives:

- **File** — the filename as it exists in the website project.
- **Original** — where known, the frame number from the original delivery
  (`stardust_ln_rough_edit-NN.jpg`), so the source file can be found. The
  website's filenames were changed to describe what is in the picture, so they
  will not match the original names.
- **Where it is used** — the page and slot, or *unused*.
- **Hero or lead?** — "Hero" means the large photograph at the very top of a
  page. "Lead" means the first large photograph on a page, below the title.
  These carry the most weight and are seen at the largest size.
- **Suggested edit** — what a final pass could improve.

**Every hero and lead image is on this list whether or not a defect was
found**, because those are the pictures seen biggest and most often.

Where an image looks clean, the entry says **"No specific defect noted —
include in the general pass."** Nothing has been invented to pad the list.

A note on two things that recur throughout the house shoot, and which are
therefore not repeated on every single entry: **windows blowing out to pure
white**, and **a warm/cool split** where the drapery reads warm cream while
the ceilings and walls read cool grey. Both are normal for daylight interiors
shot against bright windows. They are called out individually where they are
worst.

**Related file:** `docs/handoff/PHOTO-EDIT-REQUEST.md` (singular) already in this project
is a shorter, friendlier note about which frames matter most and in what
order. It is not superseded — this file is the detailed version, that one is
the priority summary.

### A1. `ivory-drapery-white-wood-blinds-bay-window-breakfast-room.jpg`
- **Original:** frame 17
- **Where used:** Ivory House project page (top photograph); also the Ivory
  House card on the homepage and on the Portfolio page
- **Hero or lead?** **LEAD** — and it is the single most-repeated Ivory House
  frame on the site
- **Suggested edit:**
  - **The left third of the frame is much darker than the rest.** The wall and
    column on the left fall away into shadow while the centre is bright.
    Evening this out is the biggest single improvement available on this
    picture.
  - **The left and right windows are blown to pure white** — the blind slats
    read as white bars with nothing behind them. Only the centre window keeps
    any greenery. The blinds are the product being sold in this photograph, so
    recovering slat and exterior detail in the outer two windows matters more
    here than almost anywhere else on the site.
  - Warm/cool split between the cream drapery and the cool ceiling.
  - The chairs and banquette are cut off along the bottom edge; if there is
    any frame below, a little more room under the table would help.

# Group B — Collected Living Room (the earlier pool-view house)

**These are not from the Ivory House shoot.** They are earlier photographs of
a different house, and they are visibly phone-camera pictures: softer, noisier
and flatter than anything in Group A. **They currently occupy the two most
important slots on the entire website**, which is why they are on this list
despite the difference in source quality.

Realistically these need reshooting more than they need retouching. But since
they are live now and may stay live for a while, a grade would still help.

  top of the site.** Also the main photograph on the Collected Living Room
