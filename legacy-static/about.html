About

Every good room starts with a story. Amy's starts in an attic.

The Founder

Amy K Clark

Amy's relationship with textiles began in her grandmother's attic in Covington, Kentucky, where turn-of-the-century trunks filled with clothing, lace, fur, texture, and detail felt like hidden treasure. Fascinated by how skilled makers could take intricate pieces apart and build them again, she taught herself to sew and began creating the things she admired but could not afford.

What began as a practical way to work while raising a family grew into decades of experience with fabric, fabrication, design, and installation. Today, Amy helps clients discover beautiful materials, solve difficult design problems, and create custom details that make a room feel personal, polished, and complete.

She still gets a little giddy over a beautiful trim. Some things do not change.

How Amy Works

Taste, technique, and a good listener

A trained eye

Amy has spent her career around fabric, proportion, and detail. She knows what a room is asking for, and she will tell you honestly when the less expensive option is the better one.

A maker's hands

Decades of hands-on fabrication and real installations mean her recommendations come from experience, not a catalog. She knows how a treatment is built, because she has built it.

A collaborative process

This is your home. Amy asks good questions, listens carefully, and brings options that fit the way you actually live, in Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky, and beyond.

Why Clients Trust Her

Real experience, real access

Clients work with Amy because she combines things that rarely come together in one person: designer-level fabric access, decades of fabrication experience, and personal, careful installation in your home.

She sources from designer fabric houses most homeowners cannot buy from directly, and she treats every home she works in with the same respect she would want in her own.

One thing she is not: a licensed interior designer running your whole home. Amy's work lives in windows, drapery, upholstery, and soft furnishings, done exceptionally well.
