Selected Projects

Drapery, shades, upholstery, and soft furnishings from homes across Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky. Every project here reflects Amy's exact scope, from fabric selection through fabrication and installation.

Drapery · Pillows

The Garden Room

Amy selected the fabric and made the floor-length drapery and coordinating pillows for this light-filled sitting room, keeping the palette soft so the garden view stays the focus.

Roman Shades · Drapery

The Riverside House

For this home overlooking the Ohio River, Amy designed and fabricated Roman shades for the kitchen and breakfast room and made drapery for the adjoining living space, balancing morning light with the water view.

Drapery · Upholstery

The Blue Study

Amy chose the fabrics, made the tailored drapery panels, and reupholstered a well-loved reading chair for this study, giving the room a calm, collected feeling without losing its character.

Whole-Home Window Treatments

The Northern Kentucky Residence

Across this Northern Kentucky home, Amy guided fabric selection and made the drapery, Roman shades, and cushions room by room, so the whole house feels considered without feeling matched.
