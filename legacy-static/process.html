Process

A calm, considered process, from first visit to finished room.

Custom work should never feel chaotic. Here is exactly how a project with Amy unfolds, whether it is one window or a whole home in Cincinnati or Northern Kentucky.

1

Consultation

Amy visits the home, understands the room, measures, and discusses priorities, function, style, and budget. This first visit is where the project takes shape: how the light moves, how the room is used, what should stay, and what has never quite worked.

2

Design and sourcing

Amy recommends treatment direction, fabrics, trims, hardware, and details with the finished room in mind. You see real materials, not just swatches on a screen, drawn from designer fabric lines and multiple manufacturers at different price points.

3

Fabrication and coordination

Every detail is specified and created or ordered for the project. Because Amy has decades of hands-on fabrication experience, nothing is specified that cannot be built well, and nothing is built without the installation already in mind.

4

Installation and finishing

Treatments are professionally installed and adjusted so the room feels complete. Hems are checked, folds are dressed, hardware is level, and the room is left tidier than it was found.

The Consultation Fee

Why the first visit has a fee

The in-home consultation gives Amy time to understand the room, discuss function and style, review possible treatments and fabrics, and take the measurements needed to recommend the right direction. The consultation fee protects the time and expertise required to give the project proper attention and is confirmed before scheduling.

The consultation fee is $125, confirmed when you schedule.
