The problems shades actually solve

Light and heat

West-facing rooms that bake every afternoon. Morning sun that makes the kitchen unusable. The right shade manages light and heat without making the room feel dark or sealed off.

Privacy without gloom

Street-facing windows and close neighbors call for privacy, but nobody wants to live behind blackout panels. Top-down options and layered treatments keep light in and sightlines out.

Too many choices

Roller, Roman, woven wood, cellular, motorized, cordless. The product landscape is genuinely confusing. Amy narrows it to the two or three options that fit your window, your room, and your budget.

Windows that are not standard

Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky are full of older homes with tall windows, deep casings, and sizes no chart anticipates. Precise measurement and correct mounting are part of the job here, not an upsell.

Why Amy

Not a commodity blind company

Big blind sellers quote a product. Amy designs a window. The difference shows up in fabric and material choices tied to the actual room, in mounting decisions that respect the trim, and in a finished installation that looks intentional rather than ordered from a catalog.

Because she works across multiple manufacturers and price points, the recommendation fits your project, not a sales target. And because she also designs and fabricates custom drapery, she knows when a shade alone is the answer and when the room wants a layered treatment.

Common Questions

Blinds & shades, answered

Do you offer blinds and shades at different price points?

Yes. Amy works with multiple manufacturers, so a project can range from practical, budget-conscious shades to fully custom fabric Roman shades. You get honest guidance on where spending more actually shows and where it does not.

Do you measure and install the blinds and shades yourself?

Yes. Professional measurement and installation are part of every blinds and shades project, so the finished treatment fits the window correctly and operates the way it should.

Which areas do you serve?

Amy K Clark Design is based in Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky and serves homes throughout the area, with select travel projects by arrangement.

How do I know whether I need blinds, shades, or drapery?

That is exactly what the in-home consultation is for. Amy looks at the light, the room, the architecture, and how you live in the space, then recommends the treatment, or combination, that fits. Many rooms end up with layered shades and drapery.
